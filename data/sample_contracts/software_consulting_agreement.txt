SOFTWARE CONSULTING & ENGINEERING AGREEMENT

This Agreement is made as of March 1, 2026, by and between CloudVentures Inc. ("Company") and DevCraft Solutions ("Consultant").

SECTION 1. ENGAGEMENT AND DELIVERABLES
Consultant shall develop backend microservices and API integrations. Consultant agrees to perform unlimited revisions without additional fee until deliverables meet the Company's sole satisfaction.

SECTION 2. INVOICING AND COMPENSATION
Invoices shall be rendered monthly. Company shall remit payment within sixty (60) days of receiving an approved invoice (Net-60). No late fee or interest shall accrue on any overdue invoice balance.

SECTION 3. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY RIGHTS
Consultant hereby assigns all right, title and interest in all software, source code, and inventions developed under this agreement on a perpetual and irrevocable basis worldwide. All work shall be considered work made for hire.

SECTION 4. INDEMNITY
Consultant shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Company from any third-party intellectual property infringement claims arising from Consultant's custom code, provided that Consultant is notified promptly in writing.

SECTION 5. CONFIDENTIALITY & NON-SOLICITATION
Both parties agree to protect proprietary technical information. Neither party shall solicit the other's employees for twelve (12) months.

SECTION 6. TERMINATION
Either party may terminate this agreement with thirty (30) days prior written notice. In the event of early termination, Consultant shall be compensated pro-rata for work completed prior to notice.
