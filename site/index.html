8-Trap Freelancer Clause Detection Specification

Engineered to catch both common industry standards and subtle predatory traps.

📜

1. IP Transfer / Work for Hire

Catches perpetual assignment before payment and lack of background tools carve-out.

⏳

2. Extended Payment Terms

Catches Net-60/90/120 terms and contingent "pay-when-paid" clauses.

🚫

3. Broad Non-Compete

Flags unreasonable durations and worldwide bans on serving other non-competing clients.

⚖️

4. Asymmetric Indemnity

Catches one-sided indemnification and uncapped consequential damages.

🔄

5. Unlimited Revisions

Spots "until satisfied" and "sole discretion" clauses without change-order billing.

🚪

6. Termination & Kill Fee

Detects immediate cancellation with zero kill fee and unpaid work-in-progress.

💸

7. Late-Payment Terms

Flags absence of overdue invoice interest and rights to withhold payment for minor disputes.

🔒

8. Exclusivity Restrictions

Identifies restrictive terms banning freelance work for any other clients.
